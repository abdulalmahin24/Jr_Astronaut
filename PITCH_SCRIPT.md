## 📋 Overview of Mandatory Video Deliverables
Per Section 08 of the official **Space Apps Challenge Participants Guide**, teams submit:
1. **Video 1 (Prescreening)**: Max **240 seconds (4 minutes)**. Concept & team qualification.
2. **Video 2 (Local Judging — "240 Seconds of Glory")**: Max **240 seconds (4 minutes)**. Full working demo + **NASA open datasets spoken aloud on camera**. Over-length videos get cut off.
3. **Video 3 (Global Judging — "30 Seconds of Glory")**: Strictly under **30 seconds**. The single video evaluated by global NASA judges.

### **BLOCK 2: WHY & THE CORE PROBLEM (0:45 – 1:30 | ~95 words)**
> **[0:45 – 1:05 | Speaker 3 - Aerospace Researcher]**  
> *"Current space education suffers from a fatal divide: it either reduces rocket science to decorative cartoons with zero authentic stakes, or buries students in dense, dry encyclopedias.*  
> *Even worse, traditional platforms force a single learning format—read a textbook, answer a quiz. But young minds learn differently: some through interactive flight, some through visual storyboards, some through audio briefings, and others through handwritten field logs."*
>
> **[1:05 – 1:30 | Speaker 1]**  
> *"And worst of all: students are never allowed to fail safely. When real mission teams encounter anomalies, they debug, adapt, and retry. In traditional classrooms, students get one shot and a grade. Jr_AstroCamp solves this by turning the mission itself into the classroom."*

---

### **BLOCK 3: WHAT & WORKING LIVE DEMO (1:30 – 3:00 | ~195 words)**
> **[1:30 – 2:10 | Speaker 4 - Game & Simulation Developer | *On-screen live demo of Pygame & Web Simulator*]**  
> *"Jr_AstroCamp features a dual-engine architecture that is 100% operational today.*  
> *First, our 60 FPS flight engine simulates 76 historical NASA missions across six flight disciplines: Launch trajectory, Orbital Docking, Lunar Landing, Rover Traverse, Space Telescopes, and Deep Space slingshots.*  
> *Watch this Apollo lunar descent: the cadet balances gravity, throttle, and thruster gimbal angle in real time. Overshoot your vertical velocity, and the lander crashes. But immediately, our debrief reveals the telemetry failure point so the cadet recalibrates and tries again."*
>
> **[2:10 – 2:35 | Speaker 5 - Full-Stack Engineer | *On-screen demo of Outpost Builder & Turn Simulation*]**  
> *"Second, our companion web portal delivers an expedition outpost builder. Cadets receive a strict 45,000 kg cargo budget on the heavy-lift booster.*  
> *Selecting heavy high-tech modules like Kilopower reactors and closed-loop Sabatier systems exceeds rocket capacity, locking launch clearance until cadets balance mass versus survival.*  
> *During the 30-Sol lunar mission, cadets navigate the brutal 354-hour lunar night, managing battery depletion, hydroponic greenhouse blights, and solar particle events."*
>
> **[2:35 – 3:00 | Speaker 2 | *On-screen demo of Multi-Format Learning Layer*]**  
> *"Around every mission sits our 4-format learning loop: illustrated comic storyboards, 90-second radio audio briefings, digital mission prediction notebooks, and real NASA archival reels. No cadet is left behind."*
